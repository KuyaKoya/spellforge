# Music Assets

Place background music files here:

- `exploration.mp3` - Exploration/room traversal music
- `normal_combat.mp3` - Normal enemy combat music
- `elite_combat.mp3` - Elite enemy combat music
- `boss_combat.mp3` - Boss encounter music

Music will automatically loop.
Volume can be controlled via `AudioManager.instance.setMusicVolume(value)`.
